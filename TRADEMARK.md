# Trademark policy

"Sluice" is a trademark of Adrian Nachev, the Project's copyright holder and
maintainer. A Benelux word mark application for "Sluice" was filed at BOIP
on 2026-08-09 (application pending, not yet registered).

## Why this exists

Neither the AGPL license in `LICENSE` nor the plugin exception in
`LICENSE-EXCEPTION.md` restricts what a fork may be named. Copyright law
governs the code. Trademark law governs the name. This document is the only
instrument here that governs the name.

## The rule

You may fork, modify, and redistribute Sluice under the terms of `LICENSE`.
If you do, you may not call your fork "Sluice." You may not use the Sluice
name, wordmark, or any confusingly similar variant as the name of your
fork, its package identifier, or its primary branding either.

This applies regardless of how much or how little of the code you change.
A fork that keeps most of the original source is still a fork. A fork that
rewrites most of it is still bound by this policy, for as long as it
carries the name forward.

## What is still fine

- Truthfully stating that your project is a fork of, based on, or derived
  from Sluice, in ordinary descriptive text (a README, a changelog, an
  about page).
- Linking to the original Sluice project or repository.
- Using the name in nominative, non-branding contexts, such as "compatible
  with Sluice's export format" or "originally forked from Sluice."

What is not fine is using "Sluice" as the name people would reach for your
fork by, the identifier under which it is packaged, distributed, or
searched.
